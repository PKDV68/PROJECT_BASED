Education & Collaborations

Project Based runs events that help students solve real-world problems, the kind that companies in the region need solved.

Every project has the same core: a real brief from a company, a team of students, and something built at the end. Some are a single event with a deadline. Others are research collaborations that run for longer.

Formats we plan to run Hackathons, datathons, product and simulation challenges, competitions and paid research collaborations

Connecting companies and students

Kreis Kleve and the surrounding regions have limited exposure to hackathon communities and few chances to collaborate with nearby companies, we bridge the gap between academia and industry.

~10,270 companies are listed in the Kleve region alone. Translation, endless possibilities of collaborations.

Working with surrounding universities and labs opens up more opportunities for students across the region.
